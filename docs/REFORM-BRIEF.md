# Proposed Reform of Student Activity Centre (SAC) Website

**Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology Allahabad, Prayagraj**

> This is the source document for every structural and textual decision on this site.
> Where the brief supplies text, that text is used verbatim. Where the brief names a
> section but supplies no text, the section is built and marked "to be supplied" —
> nothing is invented to fill it.

## 1. Vision

The redesigned SAC website should serve as the **central digital platform for student life at MNNIT Allahabad**, showcasing the Institute's vibrant co-curricular and extracurricular ecosystem.

The website should not merely provide information about SAC; it should continuously document:

* Activities being conducted
* Upcoming programmes and events
* People responsible for different activities
* Student clubs and societies
* Sports and cultural activities
* Major annual events
* Student participation and achievements
* Completed activities
* Facilities and infrastructure
* Coaches, trainers and mentors
* Awards and recognitions
* Opportunities available to students
* Photo/video documentation
* Annual performance of SAC

## 2. Proposed Main Website Structure — HOME

Hero: **STUDENT ACTIVITY CENTRE** / **Beyond Academics. Beyond Boundaries.** /
**Explore • Participate • Perform • Lead • Achieve**

Rotating banner: Annual Athletic Meet, Culrav, Avishkar, Sports teams, Club activities,
Cultural performances, Technical competitions, Yoga/Fitness activities, National-level
student achievements.

Quick statistics: **25+ Clubs | 50+ Activities | 100+ Events/Year | 5000+ Students |
XX National Awards | XX Sports Teams**. *Actual figures should be verified and updated annually.*

Homepage sections: About SAC · Latest Announcements · Upcoming Events · Activities This Month ·
Major Events · Clubs & Societies · Sports · Cultural Activities · Technical Activities ·
Student Achievements · Recently Completed Activities · SAC Facilities · Photo/Video Highlights ·
President's Message · Quick Links.

## 3. About SAC

Introduction · Vision · Mission · Objectives · Role of SAC · Administrative structure ·
Student participation model · History and evolution of SAC · Contribution towards holistic
student development.

**Core Objectives.** SAC shall facilitate opportunities for students to develop:
Leadership, Teamwork, Creativity, Technical innovation, Communication skills, Sportsmanship,
Physical fitness, Cultural awareness, Social responsibility, Entrepreneurial abilities,
Organisational and managerial skills.

## 4. SAC Organisation — People & Governance

* **President, SAC** — photograph, name, department, contact/official email, brief message.
* **Faculty Team** — for every activity: Activity | Faculty In-Charge | Faculty Coordinator | Contact.
* **Coaches & Trainers** — name, activity/sport, qualification, experience, training schedule.
* **Student Leadership** — General Secretary/Secretaries, club coordinators, captains,
  vice-captains, student executive committees.
* A separate archive should retain **Previous SAC Teams** year-wise.

Volleyball, Lawn Tennis, Kabaddi, other recognised activities.
**Indoor:** Badminton, Table Tennis, Chess, Weightlifting/Powerlifting, other recognised activities.

Each activity page: **About | Faculty In-Charge | Coach | Captain | Team | Practice Schedule |
Facilities | Events | Results | Achievements | Gallery**

## 6. Cultural & Creative Activities

Dramatics, Music, Dance, Literary activities, Fine Arts, Photography, Film/Media,
Annual Magazine – Srijan, Debate, Creative Writing, other recognised cultural groups.
Each page documents both current activities and past achievements.

## 7. Technical & Innovation Activities

Computer Coding Club, Robotics Club, Aero/Aeromodelling, Astronomy/Space, Innovation activities,
Technical societies, Emerging technology clubs.

Each club page: **About → Domains → Current Team → Projects → Workshops → Competitions →
Achievements → Resources → How to Join**

## 8. Major SAC Events

AVISHKAR (institute-level technical festival), CULRAV (cultural festival), ANNUAL ATHLETIC MEET,
JOSH, GNOSIOMANIA, and other major recognised SAC events.

Year-wise archive **2026 | 2025 | 2024 | 2023…**, each year containing: event dates, theme,
organising team, participating students, major competitions, winners, guest details, results,
photographs, videos, reports, downloads.

## 9. Completed Activities

Coordinator | Participants | Result | Photos. Creates an institutional record rather than
allowing activities to disappear after completion.

## 18. Website Navigation

**HOME | ABOUT | ACTIVITIES | CLUBS | SPORTS | EVENTS | ACHIEVEMENTS | FACILITIES | PEOPLE |
GALLERY | DOCUMENTS | CONTACT**

Under Activities: Sports · Cultural · Technical · Literary · Innovation & Entrepreneurship ·
Fitness & Yoga · Personality Development · Social/Leadership Activities.

## 19. Visual Reform

Full-width high-resolution photographs · clean typography · limited institutional colour palette ·
card-based activity presentation · responsive mobile design · fast loading · search facility ·
activity filters · event calendar · achievement counters · photo/video galleries ·
breadcrumb navigation · accessibility compliance.

Avoid long paragraphs on the homepage. Dominant style:
**Photograph + Heading + 2–3 lines + Explore button.**

## 20. Content Management Responsibility

* **President SAC** — overall approval
* **Faculty In-Charges** — verification of respective activity information
* **Club/Activity Coordinators** — submission of updates
* **SAC Web Team** — uploading and maintenance

Every completed event submitted through a standard format containing: **Event Name | Date | Venue |
Organiser | Faculty Coordinator | Student Coordinator | No. of Participants | Brief Report |
Results | Achievements | 5–10 Photographs**

## 21. Most Important Principle

The site must immediately answer: **WHAT?** (What activities does SAC conduct?) · **WHO?** (Who is
responsible for each activity?) · **WHEN?** (What is happening and when?) · **HOW?** (How can a
student participate?) · **ACHIEVEMENT?** (What has MNNIT and its students accomplished?)

The SAC website should evolve from a static information portal into a
**"Living Digital Record of Student Life at MNNIT Allahabad."**
